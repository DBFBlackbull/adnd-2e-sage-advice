Sword, Khopesh: This is an Egyptian weapon. A khopesh has about 6 inches of handle and quillons. Its blade is then straight from the quillons for about 2 feet. The blade becomes sickle-shaped at this point, being about 2 additional feet long but effectively extending the overall length of the sword by only 1 ½ feet. This makes the khopesh both heavy and unwieldy, difficult to employ properly, and slow to recover, particularly after a badly missed blow. Its sickle-like portion can snag an opponent or an opposing weapon.

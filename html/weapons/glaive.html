Glaive: One of the most basic polearms, the glaive is a sin gle-edged blade mounted on an 8 to 10-foot-long shaft. While not the most efficient weapon, it is relatively easy to make and use. Normally the blade turns outward to increase the cutting area until it almost resembles a cleaver or axe.

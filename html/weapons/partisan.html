Partisan: Shorter than the awl pike but longer than the spear, the partisan is a broad spear-head mounted on an 8-foot-long shaft. Two smaller blades project out from the base of the main blade, just to increase damage and trap weapons. Since it is a thrusting weapon, it can be used in closely packed formations.
